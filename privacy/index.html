Privacy Policy

Beat the Ad: Mini Games (Android) · Last updated: 11 November 2026

In short: your game progress stays on your phone, and if you use Google Play Games, a backup is kept in your own Play Games account. We don't ask for your name, email or contacts. The app shows ads from Google AdMob, and Google may use your device's advertising ID to show and measure those ads.

Beat the Ad: Mini Games ("the app") is made by Parthware, run by Baswaraj Raykwade ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information is used when you play.

Information we do not collect

We do not have accounts or servers for the app. Your stars, coins, levels, unlocked games, daily streak and settings are saved on your device. If you use Google Play Games, a copy of your progress is also backed up to your own Play Games account (see below). We never receive it on any server of ours. The app does not ask for your name, email address, phone number, contacts, photos, files, microphone, camera or precise location.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

When you lose a round, the app may show a full-screen ad from Google AdMob. To show ads, limit how often they appear, measure them and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising ID (app set ID on some devices),
  • approximate location derived from your IP address,
  • information about your device, the app, and how you interact with ads,
  • diagnostic information such as ad-related crash and performance data.

This information is handled by Google under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices about ads

  • Consent: where the law requires it (for example the EEA, UK, Switzerland and some US states), the app asks for your consent before personalised ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time from Privacy settings in the app.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it in your phone's Settings (Google › Ads, or Privacy › Ads, depending on the phone).
  • Avoid ads: winning a round never shows an ad, and you can also skip an ad with in-game coins.

Google Play Games (sign-in and cloud save)

If you're signed in to Google Play Games on your phone, the app uses it to back up your game progress (stars, coins, levels, unlocked games and streak) to your Play Games account, so you can restore it on a new phone or after reinstalling. The app also reads your Play Games display name to show which account is used for the backup. This backup is stored by Google in your account under the Google Privacy Policy; we don't receive it. Your phone's settings (sound, music, vibration) are not backed up.

You can stop the backup by signing out of Google Play Games, and you can delete the saved data from your Play Games account settings (Play Games app › Settings › Delete Play Games account & data, or per game).

Notifications and vibration

If you turn on the streak reminder, the app shows one local notification in the evening on days you haven't played. It is created on your phone; no data is sent to us. Vibration is used only for game feedback. You can turn both off in the app's Settings or in your phone's settings.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstalling the app (or clearing its storage in your phone's Settings) deletes all game progress from your device. A Play Games backup, if you have one, stays in your Play Games account until you delete it there. For data held by Google for advertising, use the controls above or Google's own tools.

Security

Game data never leaves your device through us. Ads are loaded by Google's SDK over encrypted connections.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date at the top.
